# Future Visual Experiments

A scratchpad of effects and refinements we might explore next to keep Ferrofluid Playground evolving.

## Colour choreography
- Palette cycles that tween magnet/particle colours through an HSV loop for a breathing effect.
- Per-particle hue offsets derived from velocity or noise, giving spikes prismatic separation.
- Life-mode gradients that ease between alive/dead palettes every few generations or bias hues by neighbour count.

## Motion + trails
- Longer persistence by blending frames with exponential decay so particles leave neon ghosts.
- Firework-style bursts or rings when the magnet engages, releases, or flips polarity.
- Field ribbons that trace recent magnet paths with soft gradient Bézier curves.

## Atmosphere & FX
- Offscreen bloom or Gaussian blur passes to amplify glow around dense clusters.
- Animated noise overlays (Perlin/FBM) to refract colours like liquid metal.
- Velocity-triggered spark sprites flickering within high-energy regions.

## Interactive twists
- Audio-reactive magnet strength, particle size, or Life update tempo based on mic/spectrum input.
- Multi-magnet support for interference patterns, each with independent colours/polarities. ✅
- Life-mode shockwaves that invert or reseed cells when the magnet toggles polarity.
- Autonomous screensaver mode with drifting magnets and palette cycling. ✅

## Presentation polish
- Curated theme presets (e.g. Solar Storm, Ethereal Lagoon, Cyber Grid) bundling colour, glow, and field settings.
- Autonomous screensaver mode that drifts the magnet and cycles Life rules/palettes over time.
